Daily Trader

Brain 3 - intraday momentum, low float, $0.50-$20. In and out the same session.

This lane is not wired to a live scanner yet, and that is a data problem rather than a code one.

Ross Cameron's edge is SELECTION - finding today's strongest mover out of thousands of names, in real time. The free Alpaca plan gives 15 real-time IEX symbols that you choose in advance, so it cannot answer "what is moving right now" at all. That is what Algo Trader Plus buys.

What DOES work today, free: the pattern engine runs over historical minute bars, because only the last 15 minutes are withheld. Measure the setup on real sessions before paying for a feed to trade it.

python bot\src\backtest_daily.py --symbols OFAL,COOL --days 20
Gates
price band$0.50 - $20.00
relative volume5x its OWN median day
session liquidity$1M traded TODAY
minimum stop5c or 0.5%, whichever is larger

Relative volume, not a 20-day average: an average test rejects every gapper on the strength of its quiet days. The stop floor exists because a one-cent stop on a $65 stock is not a tight stop, it is the spread.

Daily radar

Not built.

scan_daily.py is the next scanner. It needs a market-wide snapshot to find movers, which the current data plan cannot provide - see the note on the Overview tab. The pattern engine (daily_play.py) and its backtest are done and tested; what is missing is the thing that feeds them candidates.

Paper radar

Not built as a separate scanner yet.

Paper shares the swing brain with the Live lane, so until scan_paper.py exists it has nothing of its own to scan - it mirrors what Live finds. The difference that matters is not what it scans but what it does with it: Live stages and waits for a click, Paper fires automatically.
